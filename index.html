About this App

In a world where connections are easily lost in a cluttered phone book, CircleKeep helps you focus on your "inner circle." Whether it’s remembering a friend’s child’s name, a partner’s birthday, or a marriage anniversary, CircleKeep ensures you have the information you need at your fingertips.

What's New (v2.7)

  • 🌍 Global Localization: Full support for English, Hindi, and Spanish with localized date formats.
  • 🔍 Advanced Filtering: New multi-select mode for groups with OR/AND logic and pinned views.
  • 🎂 Smart Events: Chronological grouping (Today, Tomorrow, This Week) with early morning reminders.
  • 📲 QR Integration: Built-in scanner and generator for instant, secure contact sharing.
  • 🏠 Household Sync: Seamlessly convert partners and children into main contacts with deep-sync technology.
  • ⚙️ Performance & Stability: Optimized for Android 15 with improved R8 code shrinking and enhanced data migration reliability.

Key Features

👤

1. Comprehensive Contact Profiles

Go beyond just a phone number. Store addresses, multiple emails, company details, school/college info, and even pet names for every contact in your circle.

🏠

2. Family & Relationship Tracking

Uniquely designed to track entire households. Add partners and children to any contact record, and instantly convert them to main contacts with automated data syncing.

🎂

3. Milestone Reminders

Never miss a birthday or anniversary again. CircleKeep highlights upcoming events chronologically (Today, Tomorrow, Week, Month) and sends early morning notifications.

🔍

4. Advanced Filtering & Search

Organize your circle with multi-select groups and dynamic OR/AND logic. Pin your most important contacts or specific group views to keep them at the top.

🌍

5. Multi-Language Support

CircleKeep speaks your language. Full support for English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Spanish (Español), including localized date formats and relationship types.

📲

6. QR Contact Sharing

Share your contact details instantly with a built-in QR generator and scanner. Seamlessly import friends and family into your inner circle in seconds.

🔒

7. Data Ownership & Privacy

Your data belongs to you. Back up your entire database to a JSON file or export and share it via email. Enjoy full Light and Dark mode support and customizable privacy settings.
