Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database. We do not have access to your data.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap on Restore Purchases. This will check your Apple or Google account for the Pro Upgrade.
