Privacy Policy

CircleKeep is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data Ownership

All contact information, photos, and personal details you enter into CircleKeep are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your data, and we do not store it on our servers.

2. Data Security

Since your data is stored locally, it is as secure as your device. We recommend using device encryption and regular backups using our built-in export features.

3. Third-Party Services

CircleKeep uses Google AdMob for advertisements. AdMob may collect and use information about your usage to provide personalized ads. You can manage your ad preferences in your device settings.

4. Permissions

The app requires permission to access your contacts (for import) and camera (for QR scanning). These permissions are only used to provide the app's core features on your command.

5. Data Deletion

Users can delete all data stored within the app by selecting record(s) or by uninstalling the application from their device. For any other data-related requests, users can contact us at CircleKeepApp@gmail.com.
