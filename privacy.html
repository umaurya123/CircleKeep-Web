Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

1. Local-First Architecture

CircleKeep is designed as a local-first application. All personal data you enter—including names, contact info, photos, and notes—is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers to store your personal contact data and we never have access to it.

2. Automatically Collected Data

To facilitate app functionality and serve advertisements, we use third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: May collect identifiers (like IDFA) to personalize ads. You can opt-out in your device settings.
  • Apple/Google Billing: Handles "Pro Upgrade" payments securely. We do not see your financial details.

3. Your Rights

Because your data is local, you have absolute control. You can access, modify, or delete your data at any time within the app settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Contact Us

Questions? Contact us at CircleKeepApp@gmail.com.
